Ewemove - A fantastic 2 bedroom duplex apartment in the heart of the city centre.

This duplex apartment is located on the first floor. On entering, there is a hallway with a storage area and a storage cupboard with space for freezer. The kitchen is fitted with a gas hob, electric oven, washing machine and fridge. There are ample cupboards and an open hatch looking into the Lounge.
