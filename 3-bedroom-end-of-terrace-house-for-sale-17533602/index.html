Description

Built in 2000, this three bedroom property is tucked away at the end of a cul-de-sac with adjacent garage and driveway. It has a good sized living room to the front and a kitchen diner at the back, which opens up into the enclosed rear garden.

Externally, as it is the end plot, the garden wraps around the property, currently used for sheds and a little seating area besides the boundary trees.

The property is warmed by gas fired central heating and is double glazed throughout.
