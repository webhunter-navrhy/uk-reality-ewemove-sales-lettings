INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY ONLY - An excellent opportunity for investors to acquire a well-proportioned two-bedroom first-floor flat situated on Wessex Road, Yeovil with tenants in situ.

The property benefits from its own private entrance, with a front door and steps leading up to the first-floor accommodation. Internally, the flat offers a large and spacious living room, a modern fitted kitchen, bathroom with bath and shower over, a generous master bedroom and a separate single bedroom.

Externally, the property benefits from two brick-built storage sheds with UPVC doors, providing useful additional storage, together with communal parking.

The property is being offered strictly as an investment purchase with tenants already in situ, providing an attractive opportunity for a landlord looking to acquire a ready-made rental investment.
